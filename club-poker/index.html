Chips: how to get and exchange them

All chip operations go through the club manager at 1:1, with no fee and no wagering. It usually takes 5–30 minutes. Details are available from the manager on Telegram.

Why 50% rakeback beats any bonus

Rake is the fee the table takes from each pot (usually 5%). Classic rooms return 10–30% through levels and missions. We return a flat 50% with no conditions — over a long sample that is the difference between losing and winning. More about rakeback →
