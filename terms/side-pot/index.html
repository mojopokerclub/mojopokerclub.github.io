What is a side pot in poker

How the pot is split when players go all-in with different stacks. A three-stack example and a calculator: set the stacks and see who wins what.

All termsTake a seat
Pot and forced bets
A side pot is a separate pot created when a player goes all-in and the opponents have more chips. The all-in player can only win the main pot: from each opponent no more than they put in themselves. Everything bet on top goes into the side pot, which only the players who contributed to it can win.

Why side pots exist

In poker you cannot win more than you risk. With 20 chips against opponents betting 50, you risk your 20 and win at most 20 from each of them. The remaining 30 from each opponent is their own contest. The rule is fair to everyone: nobody hands chips to a player who did not risk the same amount.

How it is counted: three stacks

  1. Player A has 20 chips, Player B 50, Player C 100. All three go all-in.
  2. Main pot. Everyone puts in 20, the size of the shortest stack: 20 × 3 = 60, contested by all three.
  3. Side pot. B and C put in another 30, up to B’s stack: 30 × 2 = 60, contested only by B and C.
  4. Refund. C has 50 chips nobody can match. They go back to C and never enter the pot.

Who wins which pot

Each pot is decided separately: it goes to the best hand among the players who contributed to it.

  • The short stack has the best hand. It wins only the main pot; the side pot goes to the best hand among the others.
  • A big stack has the best hand. It wins the main pot and the side pot.
  • There can be several side pots. With three all-ins of different sizes and a fourth, bigger stack, you get side pot 1, side pot 2 and so on, each with fewer players.

Common mistakes

  • Thinking the all-in player with the best hand takes everything. They only get the part they could match.
  • Bluffing into a side pot against an all-in player. A bluff only works against players who can still fold; the all-in player goes to showdown no matter what.
  • Confusing a side pot with a split. A split is one pot shared equally between identical hands.
