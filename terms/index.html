Poker terms

A poker glossary from check and blinds to side pots and satellites. Short definitions and detailed explanations with card examples and calculators.

The full glossary

Bets and actions
Check
Pass without betting. Only possible if nobody has bet in this round.
Bet
The first bet in a betting round.
Call
Match the opponent’s current bet.
Raise
Increase the opponent’s bet.
3-bet
A re-raise after a raise. The next one is a 4-bet.
Fold
Throw your cards away and leave the hand.
All-in
Bet all your chips.
Limp
Entering the pot preflop by calling the big blind instead of raising.
More
Check-raise
Checking, then raising after an opponent bets.
Continuation bet
A flop bet by the player who raised preflop.
Bluff
Betting a weak hand to make a stronger one fold.
Pot and forced bets
Pot
All the chips bet in a hand. The winner takes it.
Blinds
Forced small and big blind bets before the deal.
More
Ante
A small forced bet from every player before the deal.
More
Straddle
A voluntary blind bet, usually two big blinds.
More
Side pot
A separate pot for bigger stacks when someone is all-in for less.
More
Bomb pot
Everyone posts the same amount, preflop is skipped and the flop comes at once.
More
Run it twice
The rest of an all-in board is dealt twice, each run for half the pot.
More
Split pot
A pot shared equally between identical hands.
Pot-limit
A betting structure where the maximum bet is the size of the pot.
More
Positions
Button (BTN)
The dealer seat. Acts last after the flop — the best seat.
More
Dealer button
The disc marked D that marks the dealer and sets the order of action.
More
Small blind (SB)
Left of the button, posts half the big blind.
More
Big blind (BB)
Left of the small blind, posts the table minimum.
More
UTG
First to act preflop. The earliest position.
Cutoff (CO)
Right of the button. The second-best seat.
Cards and hands
Hole cards
The face-down cards each player is dealt.
Board
The community cards: flop, turn and river.
Flop, turn, river
The first three, the fourth and the fifth community cards.
More
Kicker
The highest card outside the hand, which breaks ties.
More
Nuts
The best possible hand on the current board.
More
Set
Three of a kind from a pocket pair and a board card.
More
Trips
Three of a kind from one hole card and a paired board.
More
Draw
An unfinished hand that needs cards to make a straight or flush.
More
Outs
Cards in the deck that improve your hand to a winner.
More
Wheel
The A-2-3-4-5 straight and the best low in Hi-Lo.
More
Scoop
Winning both halves of a Hi-Lo pot.
More
Tournaments
MTT
A multi-table tournament: tables merge as players bust.
More
Buy-in
The cost of entering a tournament.
Freeroll
A tournament that is free to enter.
Satellite
A tournament whose prizes are tickets to a bigger one.
More
Bounty
A reward for knocking an opponent out.
More
Rebuy
Buying more chips early in a tournament.
More
Add-on
A one-off chip purchase at the end of the rebuy period.
More
Freezeout
A tournament without rebuys: once out, you are out.
More
Bubble
The point where one more elimination reaches the prizes.
More
Game and formats
Stack
The chips a player has.
Cash game
Play without a tournament structure: sit down and leave at any time.
6-max
A table for at most six players.
Heads-up
Playing one against one.
More
Tilt
An emotional state in which a player makes bad decisions after losses.
More
Double Board
A hand with two boards: the pot is split between the best hand on each.
More
