What is an ante in poker

How the ante differs from blinds, what a big blind ante is and how antes change the game. With a pre-deal pot calculator.

All termsTake a seat
Pot and forced bets
An ante is a small forced bet that every player at the table puts in before the deal. It comes on top of the blinds, so the pot grows before anyone bets and is worth fighting for more often.

Ante versus blinds

BlindsAnte
Who paysTwo players: small and big blindEvery player at the table
SizeThe small blind is half the big blindUsually much smaller than the big blind
Counts towards your betYes: a blind is part of your preflop betNo: the ante goes straight into the pot
Where you see itIn every gameIn later tournament levels and on some cash tables

Big blind ante

Modern tournaments often use a big blind ante: instead of everyone posting an ante, the player in the big blind pays the whole table’s ante. The pot is the same, and hands start faster.

How antes change the game

  • The pot is bigger before the deal, so opening more hands pays off, especially from late position.
  • Short stacks shrink faster: every orbit costs more than with blinds alone.
  • Blinds defend more often: there is more dead money in the pot.
  • Waiting passively for a big hand gets more expensive.
