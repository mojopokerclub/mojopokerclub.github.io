How to Create a ClubGG Account

Step-by-step guide with real screenshots from the app

1

Open the MOJO App

Launch MOJO. On the main screen tap «Join» — it opens the GGPass registration form.

2

Enter your Email

The GGPass form opens. Enter your email address and tap «Next». You can also sign in via Google, Facebook, or Apple.

3

Verify your Email

A 4-digit code will be sent to your email. Enter the code in the fields. If no email arrived, check Spam or request a new code.

Note: The code is valid for 60 seconds. If it didn't arrive, tap «Resend Code».
4

Set a Password

Create a strong password and enter it twice. Password requirements: 8–20 characters, at least one digit.

5

Create your Gaming Profile

Choose an avatar and country, then create a nickname. Requirements: 4–15 characters, latin letters, digits and [ ] _ ! - @ only. Nickname must not start with a special character.

6

Accept Terms of Service

Review the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. Check «I agree» and tap «Confirm».

7

Account Created — You're in MOJO!

Registration complete. You'll land on the main app screen. Now find the MOJO club via the search bar and send a join request.
