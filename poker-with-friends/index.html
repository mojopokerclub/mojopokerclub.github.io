How to play poker with friends online

A private table on your phone or computer in 10 minutes: your own app, your rules, only your crew. Step by step, with screenshots.

Getting friends around one poker table when everyone lives in a different city takes about ten minutes. You need an app where you can open a private table and let in only your people. Here is how it works in ClubGG — a free poker client where play runs on play chips and tables live inside clubs.

Why the club format rather than a “free poker” game from the app store: in regular games you land at a public table with strangers and ads. In a club the table is yours — you choose the game (Hold’em, Omaha), blinds, number of seats, the time, and only the people you invited sit down.

  • Play from iPhone, Android, Windows and macOS — everyone at the same table.
  • Formats: No-Limit Hold’em, PLO4/5/6, tournaments, Bomb Pot and Double Board.
  • The table lives as long as you need: one evening or a permanent “home” table.

Why ClubGG beats “home games” in regular apps

  • One table for every device. A friend on iPhone, you on Windows, another on Android — no separate versions.
  • No random players. Nobody gets to a password-protected table uninvited.
  • Chips come from the manager. No waiting for “free chips every 4 hours” — everyone starts with the same stack.
  • A full client. Multi-tabling, hand history, stats, tournaments — like a big room, but within your crew.

A tournament for your group

If you want “who wins the night” rather than a cash table, ask the manager for a private tournament: starting stack, 8–10 minute blind levels, 6–9 players. It takes an hour and a half or two and ends with a clear winner. For a bigger group a two-table Sit & Go works better.
